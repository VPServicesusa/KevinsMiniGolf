FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I play?
Drag back from your ball to set the direction and power, then release — like a slingshot. The dotted line previews your aim and how the ball will bounce. A par on each hole tells you the score to beat.
What can I do in the game?
Play the 18 signature holes, spend coins in the shop on 100+ balls and other cosmetics, build courses in the hole editor, and challenge friends in pass-and-play multiplayer for up to 6 players.
Can I play without the internet?
Yes — Kevin's Mini Golf is fully playable offline. The first time you launch with a connection it loads its display font for nicer type, but every part of the game (holes, physics, saves, and sound) works with no connection at all.
How is my progress saved?
Everything is stored locally on your device — your level progress, unlocked items, created holes, stats, and settings. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
How do I share a hole I made?
In the hole editor, use Share to generate a text code you can send to a friend. They paste it with Import to add that hole to their game. Sharing is manual and offline — there's no online feed.
How do I reset my progress?
Use Reset all progress in Settings for a clean start, or delete the app from your device to remove all of its local data. You can also begin a fresh round at any time without wiping your unlocks.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases — it's the whole game, start to finish.
Is there online multiplayer?
Multiplayer is local pass-and-play on one device, up to 6 players. There's no online play in the current release — email me if it's something you'd like to see.
What ages is it for?
Family-friendly throughout. It's bright, cartoon mini golf with no graphic content — just putting.
What devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad running iOS 16 or later. The game plays in landscape.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device

Last updated: June 2026

Kevin's Mini Golf ("the App") is a locally-stored, device-only application. The App does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data with any party.

What we store locally

The App uses your device's local storage to save:

  • Your level progress, unlocked items, and created holes
  • Your statistics, records, and achievements
  • Your in-app preferences (sound, music, theme, active player, and similar)

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to any server, and the developer has no access to it.

No third-party services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.

No purchases

The App contains no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it does not collect any personal data, it complies with COPPA (the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar laws by not collecting anything from users of any age in the first place.

Data deletion

All App data is stored on your device. To remove everything, delete the App — iOS will remove all of its associated data.
