FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I play?
Drag back from your ball to set the direction and power, then release — like a slingshot. The dotted line previews your aim and how the ball will bounce. A par on each hole tells you the score to beat.
What can I do in the game?
Play 392 holes across 18 themes, including 18 hand-designed signature ones and featured courses built by players, spend coins in the shop on 200+ balls, 160+ putters and other cosmetics, build courses in the hole editor, challenge friends online in ranked or friendly matches, and play pass-and-play multiplayer for up to 6 players on one device.
Is there online multiplayer?
Yes. Online rounds are turn-based, so nobody has to be online at the same time — take your shot, put your phone away, and get a notification when it's your turn again. Before you invite anyone you choose the number of holes and the course, and everyone picks their own ball. You can run several matches at once and see the score in each. Online play uses Game Center and needs a connection.
Who can I play online with?
Your Game Center friends appear right in Play a friend — tap Play next to one and the match starts, and they get the invite once you've taken your first shot. (iOS asks once whether the game may see your friends list; you can change that in Settings › Game Center.) You can also invite someone from Game Center's own screen, or tap Quick match and someone else looking for a game will join yours. There's no chat or messaging in the game — the only things that travel are your golf turns, emotes from a fixed set, a "your turn" nudge, and the layout of any hole you built and chose to play on.
How does ranking work?
Everyone starts at a rating of 1000. Each ranked match moves it: beat someone rated higher than you and you gain more; lose to someone rated lower and you drop further. Your first five ranked matches place you, and the tiers run Bronze, Silver (1100+), Gold (1250+), Platinum (1400+), Diamond (1550+) and Champion (1700+). With 3 or 4 players you're scored against each of the others on total strokes. Resigning or running out of time counts as a loss. You choose Ranked or Friendly when you start a match — friendly ones never change your rating. Your rating is posted to the Game Center Ranking leaderboard, so you can see where you stand among your friends and everyone else.
Can I play without the internet?
Solo rounds, pass-and-play, and the hole editor are fully playable offline — no account, no connection. Online multiplayer needs a connection and a Game Center account, and ads (on the main menu, and the optional watch-for-coins ones) load only when you're online.
How is my progress saved?
Everything is stored locally on your device — your level progress, unlocked items, created holes, stats, rating and settings. Online matches in progress are held by Apple's Game Center so you can pick them up again later, and your rating is also posted to Game Center, so a new phone picks it back up.
How do I share a hole I made?
In the hole editor, use Share to generate a text code you can send to a friend. They paste it with Import to add that hole to their game. Sharing is manual and offline — there's no online feed. You can also play your own holes in an online match; they travel with the invite, so your opponent doesn't need them.
How do I reset my progress?
Use Reset all progress in Settings for a clean start, or delete the app from your device to remove all of its local data. You can also begin a fresh round at any time without wiping your unlocks.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
There are no in-app purchases — every ball, putter, trail and theme is earned by playing. There are a few ads, kept out of the way: a small banner at the bottom of the main menu, and ads you can choose to watch for coins (in the Shop, or to double a round's coins), up to 5 a day. There are never ads while you're putting. Ads are kept to family-friendly (PG) content and aren't personalised. Saw one that wasn't right? Tap ⚑ Report an ad in Settings or the Shop.
What if someone builds a hole that isn't appropriate?
Pause the match and tap ⚑ Report this hole. Pick a reason and your mail app opens with a message to me, already filled in with a picture of the hole, the code that recreates it, and who you were playing — read it over, then send. Every report comes straight to my inbox and I look at all of them. There's no gallery or feed in the game, and a hole you receive in a match is never saved to your device.
What ages is it for?
The game is rated 9+. It's bright, cartoon mini golf with no graphic content — just putting. Online play has no chat or messaging, ads are limited to PG content and never personalised, and parents can restrict or turn off multiplayer entirely in Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions.
What devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad running iOS 16 or later. Play upright or sideways — the game turns with your device, even mid-round.

Privacy Policy

What the App keeps, and who sees it

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Kevin's Mini Golf ("the App") stores your game data on your device. The developer runs no servers, does not collect your data, and does not sell or share personal data. Three outside services can be involved, each described below: Apple's Game Center for online play, Google AdMob for ads, and your own mail app if you choose to send a report.

What we store locally

The App uses your device's local storage to save:

  • Your progress, unlocked items, coins, and the holes and courses you create
  • Your statistics, records, achievements, and ranked rating with its match history
  • Your in-app preferences (sound, music, looks, and similar)

This data stays on your device, and the developer has no access to it.

Online multiplayer and Game Center

Online play is optional, and the rest of the App works without it. If you use it, the App uses Game Center, a service operated by Apple — not by the developer. Game Center handles:

  • Your Game Center player identifier and display name, which the other players in your match can see
  • Match state — the course, scores, ball positions, your chosen look, and your ranked rating — stored on Apple's servers so a match can continue between sessions, and seen by the other players in that match
  • Leaderboard scores for Online Wins, Holes in One, and Ranking (your current rating, and how many ranked matches it took)
  • "Your turn" notifications and reminders

Friends list. If you tap Show my Game Center friends, iOS asks whether the App may read your Game Center friends list. If you allow it, the App reads your friends' names, player identifiers and pictures on your device to list them and start matches with them, and reads Game Center's Ranking leaderboard to show where you and your friends stand. Your friends list is not sent to the developer or anyone else. You can change this permission at any time in Settings › Game Center.

The developer does not collect, retain, or have access to Game Center data. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Game Center settings.

There is no chat, messaging, or free-text feature between players anywhere in the App. Names you give your own holes stay on your device — if you use those holes online, the names are replaced with generic labels before the course is sent, so no text you write reaches another player.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows a banner ad at the bottom of the main menu, and ads you can choose to watch for in-game coins. These are provided by Google AdMob, the only third-party software in the App. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and operating-system type, the App's identifier, and how you interact with the ads. How Google uses this is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • No personalised ads. The App asks Google not to personalise ads, and does not ask for permission to track you, so your device's advertising identifier is not used.
  • Family-friendly. Ads are limited to content suitable for general and parental-guidance audiences (PG), and sensitive categories are blocked.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and some US states), Google's consent form appears before any ad loads, and you can make your choices there.
  • Watching for coins is optional, up to 5 a day. Coins are only added for an ad watched to the end.

The developer receives only aggregate reports from AdMob (such as how many ads were shown) — never information that identifies you.

Reporting a hole or an ad

A hole another player built can appear in an online match. If one isn't appropriate, the pause menu has Report this hole. If you see an ad that isn't right, use ⚑ Report an ad in Settings or the Shop.

Either one opens your own mail app with a message you can read and edit before sending. A hole report contains the reason you picked, a picture of the hole and the code that recreates it, the match identifier, and the other player's Game Center display name. An ad report contains the reason you picked and Google's identifier for the ad, so it can be found and blocked. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and reports are used only to review the hole or ad.

No purchases

The App contains no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions. Coins are earned by playing, or by optionally watching an ad, and can't be bought, sold or transferred.

Children

The App is rated 9+. The developer collects no personal data from users of any age. Ads are never personalised and are limited to PG content, and online play has no chat. Parents can restrict or disable multiplayer and Game Center in Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions.

Data deletion

All App data is stored on your device. To remove it, delete the App — iOS removes all of its data. Game Center data, including match history and leaderboard scores, is managed by Apple through your Game Center account. For data Google may hold in connection with ads, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com and the policy linked above.
